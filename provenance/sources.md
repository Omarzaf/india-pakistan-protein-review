## Chapter bibliography and remaining source checks

- Bryant, Christopher, et al. “A Survey of Consumer Perceptions of Plant-Based and Clean Meat in the USA, India, and China.” *Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems* 3 (2019). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fsufs.2019.00011). Read the sample limitations before interpreting the Indian intention result.
- Other retained behavioural comparators: [Arora et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/su12114377), [Ahsan et al.](https://doi.org/10.17509/ajsee.v2i1.38766), and [Dean et al. repository record](https://hau.repository.guildhe.ac.uk/id/eprint/17927/). These are heterogeneous studies, not a representative bilateral purchase panel. Their originals still require human verification.
- Government of India, Cabinet BioE3 approval, 24 August 2024. [Official release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2048568&lang=2&reg=3). Supports stated policy intent; no country sales effect is inferred.
- Pakistan, *Pakistan Halal Authority Act*, Act VIII of 2016, 27 February 2016. [Official text](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator1d376dbb967abb1d9ed2dcb3ae978dbc.pdf). Establishment and functions support the narrow institutional statement. Later notifications and product-specific authorization were not comprehensively audited.
- Cosenza et al., [DOI 10.1002/elsc.202300005](https://doi.org/10.1002/elsc.202300005); Watson et al., [DOI 10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8). Mechanism context, not an identified national spending effect. The former's full text was not freshly recovered; no performance coefficient from it enters this model.
- Girivasan, Amrutha, and Chandana Tekkatte. *A comprehensive analysis of India's talent landscape for the smart protein sector*. GFI India. [Official landing page](https://gfi-india.org/resource/smart-protein-skills-mapping/); [PDF](https://gfi-india.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/A-comprehensive-analysis-of-Indias-talent-landscape-for-the-smart-protein-sector.pdf), physical page 10 for the 20/19 cohorts and page 27 for authors. The [official index](https://gfi-india.org/region/india/) lists 19 December 2025; the PDF itself is undated. Sampled cohorts do not establish a national workforce census.

On 22 October 2026, an agent rechecked the official GFI economic and talent originals and the two dated institutional sources. The checks supported the bounded statements above and left the Indian price/valuation assumption unresolved. They do not replace the required human verification. Full source documents and private team material are not bundled.
