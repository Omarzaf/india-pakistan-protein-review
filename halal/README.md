# Halal evidence

Two files carry the halal evidence the South Asia report draws on. Both were prepared in an earlier workstream that covered more countries than the report, and both are kept as they were checked, apart from removing links to files outside this repository and updating the status line of `phase1-review.md` on 10 November 2026.

| File                    | Date        | What it holds                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `phase1-review.md`      | 30 Sep 2026 | Phase One review: school-by-school questions, country conditions and historical comparisons for cultivated chicken                        |
| `institution-matrix.md` | 2 Oct 2026  | Seven-condition matrix across 11 named institutions and advisers, plus Javed Ahmad Ghamidi's published dietary essay as a separate author |

The report uses these files for its access conditions and process questions only. It does not use them as an adoption model, and it gives no general ruling on cultivated meat. The private interview recording discussed in the report is not included here. Qualified human review of the religious and legal readings is still pending.
