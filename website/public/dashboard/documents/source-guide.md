# Focused dashboard data guide

This public snapshot contains seventeen retained aggregate or diagnostic tables. The source register is limited to the 65 identities referenced by these tables and the proxy source ledger. Numerical values, numerical strings, status flags, precision and missing cells remain unchanged. Internal raw-file and calculation-file storage routes are excluded; source IDs, public bibliographic links, hashes and table/page/cell locators remain available.

## Measurement boundaries

- India per-person intake and energy-equivalent consumer-unit estimates use separate denominators.
- Pakistan complete protein intake remains missing. Native monthly food quantities do not become complete nutrient intake.
- Food frequency, production, supply, expenditure and alternative-protein spending scenarios are separate measures.
- Source-specific periods, survey populations, recall windows, units and vintages must accompany comparisons.
- Proxy p10/p50/p90 values summarize chosen assumptions. Panel quantiles summarize accepted response medians. Neither provides a calibrated national adoption probability.
- Household scenario, sensitivity and holdout records are frozen historical diagnostics. Pakistan household totals remain held.

## Source access and review

Source metadata identifies original publications; it does not establish current accessibility or grant a licence to their full texts or underlying databases. No source PDF, workbook, respondent record, provider transcript or questionnaire is republished by this dashboard. Human factual/code and scholarly review remain pending. Bibliographic rights notes remain source-specific.

The original broad data guide also describes tables outside this focused export. Those tables are not included here.
