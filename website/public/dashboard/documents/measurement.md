# Measurement contract and proposed harmonization

**Proposed for review, 22 October 2026. No team agreement is asserted.**

| Strand | Outcome / channel | Technology and category | Geography / time | Uncertainty meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regional proxy | Intended domestic retail spending; the Indian source's retail basis is assumed | Plant-based replacement foods plus fermentation-derived human foods; no cultivated meat; aggregate anchor limits category detail | India/Pakistan; 2022 Indian estimate converted to 2025 prices; 2026/2030/2035 scenarios | Percentiles under analyst-selected parameter distributions; no demonstrated coverage |
| Regional panel | Headline retail spending; excludes foodservice and conventional staples | Plant-based replacements, all dairy alternatives and other included plant-based replacements; headline excludes fermentation/cultivated technologies | India/Pakistan; 2026/2030/2035; million market-exchange-rate USD at 2025 prices | Percentiles of ten accepted response medians by requested model; not predictive bounds |
| Separate panel US arm | Original all-category plant-based retail question; additional technology questions have their own scope | Broader US plant-based basket, with distinct cultivated and fermentation questions | US methodological comparator in the author's supplement | Same descriptive run-median aggregation; prompts and supplied evidence differ by region |
| Historical US benchmark | GFI/SPINS observed retail sales | Broad basket including prepared meals | Fixed 2025 observation; per-person denominator | Source estimate; not a 2035 US forecast |
| Conventional food evidence | Supply balance or reported household food quantities | Animal foods and conventional categories | Source-specific year, sample and geography | Measurement/sampling properties; does not establish alternative-protein purchases |

For any other contribution to the unified report, record the actual executed question, product basket, channel, currency, price year, source vintage and uncertainty measure before comparing it. A common label introduced after collection does not make different protocols equivalent.

The main research question is what the evidence supports about demand and what would reduce decision-relevant uncertainty. Keep stated willingness, paid first purchase, repeat purchase and animal-food displacement separate. Model output, policy intent, company activity and missing data are different evidence classes. Do not sum regional results into a global market, pool proxy draws with model responses, or attribute disagreement to one cause without a design that identifies it.
