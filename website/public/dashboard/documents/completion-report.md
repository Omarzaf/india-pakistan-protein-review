forecast: the repository can support a reproducible measurement audit and
conditional scenarios. A stronger empirical contribution needs linked
pre-purchase intentions and verified subsequent transactions in a defined
regional cohort. The study is designed to reveal low conversion as readily as
high conversion.

causal or national interpretation is implied.
