# Remaining method questions

Prepared for author or qualified survey-method review. These questions have not
been sent to MoSPI, PBS or anyone else. No access application was submitted.

## India — what would resolve the point baseline

1. Obtain the production syntax or an exact specification for the **unadjusted**
   protein column of HCES Report 594 Table 3.18R/U. Does it use food-item cooked
   meals or the FDQ-member roster's `X+G` construction? The report explicitly
   documents the adjusted `X+G*` formula; numerical closeness cannot establish
   the unadjusted one.
2. Obtain the exact rural/urban CPI family and multipliers used to convert
   December 2024 rupee-based nutrient factors to August 2023–July 2024 prices.
   General CPI and Food and Beverages remain separate declared interpretations.
3. Obtain release-specific rules for blank away-meal fields and absent food-item
   records. Separately, an adjusted estimator requires the rule for `A+B=0`.
   FDQ membership itself is now resolved by the official layout and candidate
   counts; it does not resolve those blanks.

## Pakistan — what would resolve the point baseline

1. Obtain the exact Table 23 production recipe: 14-day-to-month conversion,
   eligible population and denominator, all four acquisition channels, and any
   release processing or exclusions. Generic ratio-estimation equations and an
   annual expenditure check do not establish this quantity-table algorithm.
2. Obtain a version/correction mapping between the economic report, standalone
   workbook and released Section 6 data. Identify which bytes produced the
   published national 0.34, rural 0.29 and urban 0.41 values.
3. Obtain the meaning of an absent chicken-item record and null acquisition
   channels. The released data contain 7,215 households without a chicken row.
   They remain in the population denominator; no unobserved quantity is filled.

## Separate research-readiness requirements

- India: acquire the official raw release through its permitted route and replay
  cleaning with logged conversions and joins. Matching layout offsets and
  candidate hashes does not establish raw-record fidelity.
- Both countries: establish the final variance strata/substrata, weights and
  singleton treatment before presenting certified design-based uncertainty.
- Keep author/scholarly approval and permission to redistribute data separate
  from computational verification.

The next useful work is an answer to a specific question above, supported by
production syntax, a release note or authoritative method documentation. Another
unbounded search or a formula selected because it matches would not resolve the
current acceptance problem.
