# Limitations and AI-use statements

**Local review candidate, 27 October 2026. Human verification pending.**

## Main-text limitations

The India–Pakistan results are conditional spending scenarios. The Indian anchor is an external modeled estimate with an unstated price and valuation basis; the analysis assumes nominal 2022 rupees and retail value. Pakistan lacks a comparable verified national baseline in the retained evidence. Proxy coefficients and distributions are analyst choices, with possible overlap among income, urbanisation, animal-protein supply and industry activity. The reported simulation percentiles have no demonstrated probability coverage. Macro projections beyond 2031 extend the last available growth rates. The US comparison uses a different product basket and a 2025 observation rather than a matched forecast-year benchmark. National supply, stated intention, paid purchase, repeat purchase and animal-food displacement remain different outcomes. The source collection is selective and does not establish that evidence absent from this collection is absent everywhere.

The separate model-elicitation supplement is a repetition and disagreement exercise under recorded workflows. Its regional prompts, evidence conditioning, execution dates, tools and technology scope can differ from other contributors' studies. Model versions and attempted-run selection also matter. The accepted set excludes rejected and errored attempts, and a parser rule changed during collection. The spread of run medians is not a predictive interval. Similar answers do not prove calibration or independent evidence; different answers do not isolate the effect of a model, prompt or AI assumption. The supplement is authored as Umar's work and is not represented as an accepted joint result.

## Limits of the GFI comparison and revised recommendations

The GFI comparison is an inverse-model diagnostic under an unresolved source price basis. Original rupee values accompany conditional dollar conversions. A dash in GFI's fermentation baseline remains undefined, not an observed zero. Mechanical factorization does not identify causal contributions to the disagreement. The chosen model bounds can preclude GFI's targets; that alone does not make either forecast empirically correct. GFI supplied the proxy baseline and forward scenarios in the regional elicitation packs, so these strands are not independent validation.

Changing the US-derived growth floor or fixing elasticity probes sensitivity; neither is an estimated South Asian correction. Pakistan remains a transfer without a verified national sales anchor. A driver with no direct national evidence is labelled as such. Proposed price, repeat-purchase, certification, technical and availability tests have no established impact or funding ranking. The original US panel's three themes remain reviewer-supplied comparison prompts until its actual executed study and author scope are verified; they are not pooled with the separate 60-run supplement.
